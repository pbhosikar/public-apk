SevenMentor WhatsApp Sync

Paste your CRM API URL and the pass key from CRM → WhatsApp Chrome Sync. Then open WhatsApp Web and leave this extension enabled.

  1. Open web.whatsapp.com and scan QR once on this PC.
  2. Open a lead chat that matches a CRM phone number.
  3. Messages upload automatically; check the lead WhatsApp tab in CRM.
